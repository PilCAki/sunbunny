SUNBUNNY PRESS COPY

Chicago indie psych with jangly guitars and jazz harmony.

RELEASE DESCRIPTION
Sunbunny’s forthcoming album arrives January 29, 2027. Chicago guitarist and songwriter Phillip Adkins brings vocals back into focus in a more laid-back set of songs built around pop melodies, jangly electric guitars, and jazz harmony. Guitar and bass lines move around the voice as the rhythms shift beneath them.

“Listen to Me” is a quick-moving psych-pop track from the album, with jangly guitars and a prominent melody.

ARTIST BIO
Sunbunny is the bedroom recording project of Chicago guitarist and songwriter Phillip Adkins. He builds songs around pop melodies, using jazz harmony, interlocking guitar and bass lines, and shifts in rhythm to take them somewhere less familiar.

Listener feedback on “Listen to Me”.
“Wildly avant-garde, psychedelic, lo-fi rocker with trippy carnival vibe that reminds quite a bit of Syd Barrett, especially the jangly, heavy, intricate guitar flourishes. Spacey vocals add to this vibe.”
