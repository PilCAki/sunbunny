Sunbunny

Psychedelic indie jazz noise pop from a Chicago guitarist, pedal addict and sound designer. The new album … and Other Suburban Terrors is out January 29, 2027.

Hear the catalog

Sunbunny builds whole records out of three tracks and a room full of effects pedals.

A Chicago guitarist and harmony nerd raised on jazz and impressionist classical, obsessed with vintage gear and perpetually chasing guitar sounds nobody has found yet. Most Sunbunny records start the same way: one track each of guitar, bass and drums, usually improvised in a single take, pushed through a somewhat unreasonable chain of hipster flavor-of-the-month effects pedals. When the need to push beyond what pedals can provide arises, Sunbunny builds custom audio plugins. Five albums and a pile of singles since 2020.

2027

… and Other Suburban Terrors

Out January 29. Sunbunny stripped back raw to a homemade guitar straight into overloaded vintage amps for thick dry old-school tones with a focus on tight songwriting and exuberant arrangements.
