What It Does

  • RMS & LUFS-maxxing extreme clipper — designed to push your mix to +3 LUFS and higher.
  • Input EQ (Lo + Hi) — shape the signal before clipping with adjustable frequency and gain controls.
  • Output EQ (Lo + Hi) — sculpt the clipped tone with a second EQ stage after the clipper.
  • Auto Gain normalization — automatically compensates for loudness changes so you hear the tone, not the volume.
  • LUFS Boost — push loudness with a dedicated LUFS-aware boost control.
  • Comprehensive metering — rail percentage, LUFS short-term & momentary, true peak, RMS, and peak hold displays.
  • Oversampling — selectable oversampling for cleaner clipping at high gain settings.
  • Zero latency — no look-ahead, no latency. Smash in real time.
